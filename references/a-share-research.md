# A-share disclosures, accounting and question themes

Use for mainland-listed equities. Confirm the exact security, exchange, currency and local trading calendar; A/H shares are separate price series. Apply the shared five-year default and attribution grades.

## What was public before the move?

Prefer dated exchange/CNInfo filings, company investor-relations records, official product channels and contemporaneous original reporting. Search Chinese company names, ticker and relevant product/industry terms. Use secondary reports to locate originals and retain access limits when the original is unavailable.

Distinguish reporting-period end, board approval, file date, first public release, meeting time and later transcript publication. A board meeting is not proof that results were public then. A next-day PDF date may coexist with a verified prior-evening release; preserve both and cite the evidence. Do not infer after-close timing merely because earnings usually appear in the evening. Use unknown timing and sensitivity when necessary. Check exchange holidays and the last completed session rather than using today's date as a price date.

A company response released after the close cannot explain an earlier intraday move as new information. Keep the underlying rumor/report and the later clarification as separate nodes. Announced plans, approvals, actual execution and resulting operating outcomes are different stages. Apply this to production ramps, buybacks, placements, shareholder disposals and overseas listings. Authorization or sale eligibility is not execution; a buyback for employee plans is not automatically permanent share cancellation. A regulatory proposal is not an enacted blanket ban: inspect status, jurisdiction and affected activities.

## Standalone quarters and prior expectations

Label currency and units explicitly, commonly CNY yuan or CNY 100 million. Preserve revenue, attributable net income, attributable income excluding non-recurring items, operating cash flow and EPS definitions.

- Use directly disclosed standalone-quarter figures where available. For additive flow metrics, derive Q2 as H1 minus Q1 and Q4 as full year minus nine months only after checking consolidation scope, accounting basis and restatements. Show the formula and sources; reconcile back to the cumulative total. A later annual quarterly table is a restated historical series when applicable, not automatically what investors first saw.
- Do not subtract cumulative EPS to create standalone EPS: weighted-average share counts may differ. Keep unavailable single-quarter EPS blank unless it can be calculated from matching income and a verified quarter-specific denominator. Preserve split/restatement basis.
- Separate company earnings forecasts (业绩预告), preliminary results (业绩快报), formal reports and historical analyst consensus. Earlier disclosures can make later reported growth already known. Compare the same metric and period; an annual profit range is not a Q4 consensus estimate. Keep historical consensus and surprise unavailable when missing.
- Where profit and cash diverge, examine receivables, inventory, prepayments, capital spending and collection timing. A management explanation remains management-reported, and a cash-flow gap alone is not proof of fraud or price causation.

## Question-focus migration

Use public investor-relations activity records (投资者关系活动记录表), results briefings, investor meetings and earnings-call transcripts. Do not imply that every A-share company has a US-style quarterly sell-side call.

For each sample show meeting date, reporting period, audience type, source and question/page locator, question theme, management answer, change relative to the previous **read sample**, and the next observation that could confirm or refute the answer. Preserve named analysts only when identified by the source; an attendee list does not assign each question to a particular analyst. Label anonymous questions as institutional/investor questions.

Disclose missing quarters and differences between institutional calls and public online briefings. Qualitative theme changes are not question-frequency statistics, consensus or evidence that an older concern disappeared. A hypothetical number inside an investor's question is not company guidance.

## Review examples

- A stock jumps before its evening preliminary-results call: investigate earlier peer results and other news; calculate the company's response window from the later disclosure.
- H1 operating cash flow is positive but less than Q1: Q2 cash flow is negative. Check cash-conversion explanations without inferring wrongdoing.
- A public briefing asks about a possible order volume: keep it as a question unless management confirms the number.
- A large ETF price drop coincides with a unit split: validate the adjustment before selecting it as a market event, using [event-windows.md](event-windows.md).

These are research instructions, not automated collection or accounting functions in the bundled app.
